Brotato RL

A reinforcement-learning environment for Brotato (Demon, Danger 5, endless mode), built in a 12-hour hackathon with PufferLib 5. Code: github.com/IlyaasK/brotato-rl.

A 1B-step Demon policy playing the simulator (puffer eval).

What it is

  • Simulator: a C reimplementation of the game that runs on CPU and CUDA. GPU and CPU results are bit-identical on every state field.
  • Checked against the real game: a Godot mod drives the real game headless and records every frame. The simulator replays those recordings and has to match them value for value.
  • Full action space: 31 actions covering movement, buying weapons, items, pets and structures, reroll, lock, ban, recycle, combine, level-ups and crates, with a per-phase action mask. The observation has 203 values.
  • Training speed: about 1.8M env steps/s on an RTX 5070 with 4,096 environments.
