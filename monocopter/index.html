▼info✕
Joshua Bird's single-propeller drone flies on one hinged rotor, tilting by pulsing motor torque once per revolution. A PufferLib PPO policy (2-layer MinGRU, 64 units, ~200M steps, RTX 5070) replaces the flight controller's attitude PIDs and holds it over the cyan target. Physics and policy run live in WebAssembly.
Simulation with randomized, unmeasured physics. Not flight-tested.
