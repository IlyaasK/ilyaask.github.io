Monocopter

A neural network policy flying a simulated single-propeller drone, live in your browser

Joshua Bird's single-propeller drone flies on one hinged rotor: it tilts by pulsing motor torque once per revolution, so the blades change pitch around the disc. Here a PPO policy replaces the flight controller's attitude PIDs. It outputs the same throttle/roll/pitch commands ArduPilot sends to the moteus motor controller and must hold the drone over the cyan target.

Every episode is a different randomized drone: mass, inertia, thrust, blade response, phase error, battery voltage and command latency are sampled from wide ranges, because the real vehicle's values have not been measured yet. This is a simulator result, not a flight-tested controller.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. The camera follows one drone; the red dots are 15 others.

Policy: 2-layer MinGRU, 64 hidden units, about 200M steps of PPO in PufferLib 5.0, best of a 100-trial hyperparameter sweep; trained on an RTX 5070. Over 3,000 evaluation episodes it scores 0.68 on a 0–1 hover-quality metric (the untuned 1B-step baseline: 0.65).
