Bounce

The 2001 Nokia classic, played by an AI trained with PPO for 553 million steps

The original 2001 Nokia Bounce — all 11 levels, reverse-engineered from the JAR — running entirely in your browser via WebAssembly. The red ball is controlled by a neural network policy trained with PPO, currently clears Level 1; full 11-level training is in progress.

Environment: C rewrite of the JAR game logic, 126-dim observations.
Training: 553,648,128 timesteps, PPO, RTX 5070.
