Environments, and the speed they train at

Brotato RLsim + policy

1.8M steps/s · RTX 5070

Twelve hours at a hackathon: I stripped the Godot engine out of Brotato and wrote a bit-exact C simulator with the physics and contact solving parallelised in CUDA — 4,096 environments on one card at about 1.8M env steps/s, against roughly 1,200 steps/s for the shipped game. The CPU and CUDA builds agree on every state field, and a Godot mod drives the real game headless so I can check my frames against its frames.

a 1B-step policy, in my simulator
