Here is the 1.0 version of the Minekart Madness NES ROM, as it was submitted to the nesdev compo 2022.

The original game targets a fairly rare mapper, Mapper 28, so I have also included an MMC1 Mapper version of the game, which is compatible with every emulator every made pretty much.
