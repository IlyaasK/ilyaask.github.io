nes-core

A 2,600-line emulator I wrote from the NESdev wiki without looking at any other emulator, never tuned against the corpus. The NES counterpart of GBA Eval's gba-core. Under v1.0 it scored 0.5811: most of its replay loss was the same unstated first-frame convention the DeepSeek run fitted. v1.1 states the convention in the ABI, and with that one line it scores 0.9426 (details).
