nes-core

A 2,600-line emulator I wrote from the NESdev wiki without looking at any other emulator, graded once and never tuned against the corpus. The NES counterpart of GBA Eval's gba-core. Most of its replay loss turned out to be the same unstated first-frame convention the DeepSeek run fitted: with a one-line change it scores 0.9426 (details).
