DeepSeek V4.1 Flash

Replays real games almost perfectly (four of six score 0.9998 or better) but fails five test ROMs outright: the four Holy Mapperel board tests and blargg's official-opcode suite. One constant in its frame loop, fitted to the reference, is worth 0.36 of its score; see the post on reward hacking.
