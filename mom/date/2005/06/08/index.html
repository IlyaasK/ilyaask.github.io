My Gratitude Journal

June 8, 2005

Assalaamualaykum!
What am I grateful for today?

  • For my 4 month old son,Ilyaas. He is the greatest blessing I have received, the best thing that can happen to me.
  • For having my mother with me, a huge help with Ilyaas.
  • For having a roof above my head, there are many who are homeless.
  • For my laptop, I think i would be a handicap without it.
  • For my internet connection.

Its sad how we overlook ALLAH’s blessings and focus on the things we don’t have. Allah has said in the Qur’an ” LA IN SHAKARTUM , LA AZEEDANNAKUM” …{If you give thanx, surely we will increase for you.}

Now, it’s your turn, what are you grateful for today?
