Summer Course

June 22, 2005

Assalaamualaykum wa Rahmatullah wa Barakaatuhu,

The Summer Vacations are about to begin in a few days…Most of the students would be thinking to themsselves, “Phew! A long awaited rest.” Mothers would probably be worrying,”Lord, Help Me! How am I gonna handle them all day, These kids of mine will wreak havoc…”

Well, What are you upto this Summer? Many of us intend to travel, What about the others? Have u made any plans? If the reply is, “Nah! Gonna hog and sleep and enjoy these moments of peace…” , I would like to remind you :

“So when you have finished (your occupation), devote yourself for Allah’s worship. And to your Lord (Alone) turn ( all your ) intentions and hopes.” Surah Ash-Sharh, Verses 7 -8.

Now that we have some free time on our hands, this is the apt opprtunity for us to invest in our spiritual future…our Never-Ending future…our True Future…
I can already hear cries of “Oh no! The Summer is my only chance to chill out and have fun…I’ve been working hard and I deserve a break!”
Well, We all have to keep on working untill we reach our graves…Only then can we obtain a “break”. We definetely don’t wanna be with those who regret then…
So start investing in your spiritual future, You will only reap what you sow, Let this summer cut out all your excuses!

Enroll your kids for Islamic Programs, Summer Camps, Courses…Don’t delay or you’ll be put on the waiting list…
Engage yourself in Islamic activities as well…Take a course, Volunteer for an Islamic Cause, Teach Islam to your own children and the neighbours’…there are countless options…

I am unaware of the Summer programs being held in your area, but I reside in Bahrain and I would like to invite you to one such Course to be held here, If u live in Bahrain, and are female, make sure you don’t miss it!

Dar Al Iman, Gudaibiya is offering Women of all ages a Comprehensive Islamic Course this Summer. In it you will be able to learn the Translation and Explanation of Surah Al-Hujurat…an indispensible Surah which lays down a general code of Behaviour and a set of moral ideals for Muslims as well as all mankind. Moreover, you can also learn Basic Qur’anic Arabic Grammar which aids one in the undertanding the Qur’an better. Students will also be taught Ahadeeth and Dua’s.
The duration of the course is from 9th July to 24th July.
Interested ones can enroll their names by calling 17717097 . Alternatively, you can email your Name, Address and Telephone Number at daraliman@hotmail.com

More information can be obtained at 17717097 or by emailing daraliman@hotmail.com

Spread the word, and invite your daughters, friends, mothers, sisters, wives, colleagues, and your near and dear ones to join.If you aren’t in Bahrain, but know someone that is, please let them know.
Hope to see you there!
Fi Amaan ALLAH.
