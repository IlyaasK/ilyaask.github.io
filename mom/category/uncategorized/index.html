Setting Goals

December 19, 2005

Assalaam ‘Alaikum wa Rahmatullah!
Reading Goals-1 and Goals-2 has motivated me a lot…
Forming Goals in our lives and striving to attain those goals is indispensable to a person’s life.
A life without a purpose, a goal is a life not worth living…Setting certain goals and then strategiic planning helps us in accomplishing those goals..Hard work is another factor which leads to success.
It’s ironical how we set goals for the worldly life, but when it comes to our eternal, never ending life, we are unsure of what direction to steer to…Most goals are confined to the realm of this world, we all want to have a good education, a big home , the latest cars and ofcourse we also want the very best for your kids…
But do we even have a goal for our after life? Sadly, many of us are so occupied with bettering our lives in this temporary abode that we often overlook and completely forget where we are heading…
The above mentioned artocles are very thought provoking, after reading them, I knew I had to set a certain goal which would contribute to securing me a place in JANNAH…Jannah is what I seek so desperately and every Muslim should too… I decided to pursue and undertake Qur’an memorisation with a firm resolve…I made some calculations, and if I even memorised 1/2 a page per day, I would, insha ALLAH, with His help, be able to finish memorising the whole Qur’an in 5 years time….and all it would take would be approximately 1/2 an hour a day,That much I can do, that much I should do, if I really seek Jannah…
We all ought to set either long term or short term goals to enhance and enrich our spiritual soul and to better our after Life which is after all, our true Life…Life on the earth is merely a test and we certainly cannot afford to engross ourselves with our wordly lives when our eternity is at stake, no sane person can justify doing that and yet, we all, are doing just that and regettably, without even realising that!
Some other goals we can pursue:
  • Praying on time if we are of those who delay our Salah or are negligent of it.
  • Concentrate on acquiring enough wealth(ofcourse earned my halal means) so we can build a Masjid, and let it be a Sadaqah Jariyah on our part rather than collecting the money to buy the latest car.
  • Try to learn and understand what the Holy Qur’an says(learning the translation), giving ourselves a year’s deadline.
  • Try to make a resolve about going for Hajj the next year.
  • Leave smoking within the next six months.
  • Memorise Qur’an.
  • Start wearing Hijab.
  • Work for Islam, engaging in Da’wah, helping people revert to Islam, that is the second most coveted goal for me.
  • Resolve to make our children good Muslims and for that we have to be their role models, thus we are required to arm ourselves with proper Islamic Knowledge and actions and learn how to instill Islamic values in our kids.

Ofcourse, these are just a few of the many, if we take it upom ourselves to accomplish our Islamic goals within a set period of time, we can succeed in changing our lives for the better, and Insha ALLAH, in the process, change the lives of those around us too.

May ALLAH guide us to The Straight Path! Ameen!

Here is a Ramadan recipe I’d like to share:

BANANA AND DATE CRUMBLE

Ingredients

2 tsp butter( 40 grams)
4 bananas, peeled and sliced (600 grams)
2 cups dates, stone removed (265 grams)
1/4 cup sweetened condensed milk (50 ml)
1/2 tsp cinnamon powder (3 grams)

Crumble Ingredients

1 cup bread crumbs (120 grams)
1 cup oat ( 85 grams)
1/4 cup brown sugar (43 grams )
4 tbsp butter ( 80 grams)

Method

  • Saute bananas with butter in a saucepan and then add the dates, milk and cinnamon and stir.
  • Remove the mixture from heat and place in a square baking dish ( 23 cm/ 9 inch).
  • Mix the crumble ingredients and cover the date mixture with it.
  • Bake at 150 degrees (300 degrees Farenheit) gas mark 3 for 20 minutes.
  • Serve warm.

Enjoy!

Note :

During Ramadan, many women spend more than the usual time in their kitchens preparing an extensive meal. This is not what Ramadan is all about, Ramadan is about connecting yourself to Allah, about engaging in Ibadah, about puriying your soul, so sisters pls keep this in mind and try to minimise your time in the kitchen. Brothers, ease the burden on your mothers, wives, sisters, by helping out a lil bit ( Don’t consider it a belittling task, it was the practice of the Prophet Muhammad, sallallahualayhiwasallam to help out in the household chores and do all his work by himself) and not making too many demands and being content with what has been served.

Give them that which is best in this world, the best in the hereafter, and Protection from the fire. You don’t know what you can do until you try.Nothing will come of nothing. If you don’t go out on a limb, you’re never going to get the fruit. There is no failure except in no longer trying. Hazy goals produce hazy results. Clearly define your goals. Write them down, make a plan for achieving them, set a deadline, visualize the results and go after them. Just don’t look back unless you want to go that way.Defeat may test you; it need not stop you. If at first you don’t succeed, try another way. For every obstacle there is a solution. Nothing in the world can take the place of persistence. The greatest mistake is giving up. Wishing will not bring success, but planning, persistence and burning desire will.There is a gold mine within you from which you can extract all the necessary ingredients. Success is an attitude. Get yours right. It is astonishing how short a time it takes for very wonderful things to happen.
