Calibration

Each patch is applied to a clean Super Mario 64 decompilation tree and graded like a submission: known hardware speedups must pass and score monotonically, while known shortcuts (-ffast-math, muted audio, low-poly Mario, halved physics steps, harness tampering) must be rejected.
